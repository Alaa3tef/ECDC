EYE WASH STATION CHECK
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.eyeStatus}}
Inspection Instruction :
  • Step 1:Check the station for damage, leaks, or corrosion. Make sure it is accessible, uncluttered, and marked.
  • Step 2: Check the water quality and level. Replace it if it is dirty, discolored, or smelly. Flush the water for 20 seconds if it is plumbed
  • Step 3: Test the station by activating it with one motion. The water should flow from both nozzles with the same pressure and cover a large area. The dust covers should pop off automatically. The water should be tepid, not too hot or cold.
  • Step 4:Check the nozzles for cleanliness and function. The nozzles should be free of dust, debris, or corrosion.The water should spray softly and gently. The nozzles should be aligned at the same height and angle.

SCBA INSPECTION
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.scbaStatus}} 30Min: {{WeeklyInspectionObj.scba30min}}
15Min: {{WeeklyInspectionObj.scba15min}} 10Min: {{WeeklyInspectionObj.scba10min}}
Inspection Instruction :
    Step 1: Check the exterior of the breathing apparatus for any signs of damage, leaks, or corrosion. Make sure the straps, face-piece, valves, and cylinders are intact and secure
    Step 2: Check the pressure in the cylinders. Make sure they are fully charged and have enough air supply for the intended use
    Step 3: Check the volume flow and the quality of the air supplied in the breathing apparatus. Make sure the air is clean, clear, and free of contaminants. If the breathing apparatus is a rescue type, make sure the air is compatible with the atmosphere of the rescue site
    Step 4: Test the operation of the breathing apparatus by donning it and performing a fit check. The face-piece should seal tightly against the face and the breathing circuit should function properly. Test the alarm, the regulator, the control module, the power module, and the HUD if applicable
    Step 5.Record the date and initials of the person who performed the inspection on a log sheet or a tag. If any issues are found, contact the maintenance department or the manufacturer for repairs or replacements. Notify the supervisor or the manager of the inspection results and any corrective actions taken

6 WELDING EQUIPMENT
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.weldingStatus}}
Inspection Instruction :
    1. Maintain a minimum 6-meter separation between oxygen and fuel-gas cylinders.
    2. Store unused cylinders with hand-tightened valve covers.
    3. Equip oxygen and acetylene cylinders with flashback prevention devices.
    4. Store cylinders in a cool, dry area away from heat sources.
    5. Secure cylinders upright during storage and transportation.
    6. Check labels for accuracy and legibility.
    7. Inspect valves for damage, corrosion, and leaks.
    8. Verify regulator matches gas type and pressure rating.
    9. Check regulator threads for damage and proper seal.
    10. Inspect hoses and fittings for wear, cuts, and leaks.
    11. Confirm pressure relief devices are functional.
    12. Check power cords/plugs, ensuring proper grounding.
    13. Inspect electrode holder, welding cables, and ground clamp for damage.
    14. Verify welding helmet and protective gear are in good condition.
    15. Confirm the availability and functionality of fire extinguishers.
    16. Ensure proper ventilation in the work area.

Smoke Detector
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.smokeStatus}}
Inspection Instruction :
    1. Test alarm: Press the test button to confirm the alarm functions.
    2. Warning lights: Check for indicator lights and refer to the manual for interpretation.
    3. Check power source: Ensure the detector has batteries or a backup battery.
    4. Battery replacement: Change batteries annually or per manufacturer's recommendation.
    5. Clean detector: Use a vacuum cleaner to remove dust and debris.
    6. Obstructions: Ensure no objects block the detector.
    7. Placement: Confirm proper installation in bedrooms, hallways, and on each level.
    8. Expiry date: Replace detectors after 10 years or as per the expiration date.

Fire Extinguishers
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.fireExtinguishersStatus}}
Inspection Instruction :
    1. Check pressure gauge: Ensure the pressure gauge indicates the fire extinguisher is charged and ready.
    2. Inspection tag: Confirm the extinguisher has a current inspection tag.
    3. Verify accessibility: Ensure the extinguisher is easily accessible and not obstructed.
    4. Check for physical damage: Inspect for dents, leaks, or other visible damage.
    5. Inspect hose/nozzle: Ensure the hose or nozzle is in good condition and free from blockages.
    6. Confirm seal and pin: Check that the tamper seal is intact, and the pull pin is securely in place.
    7. Examine the label: Ensure the label provides proper instructions and hasn't faded or become illegible.
    8. Weigh the extinguisher: Confirm the weight is within the specified range as indicated on the label.
    9. Shake extinguisher: Periodically shake dry powder extinguishers to prevent powder settling.
    10. Employee training: Ensure personnel know the location and proper use of fire extinguishers.

Dropped Inspection
Status : {{WeeklyInspectionObj.droppedInspectionStatus}}
Inspection Instruction :
    1. Identify all potential dropped object hazards in the mast area.
    2. Ensure all equipment and tools are properly secured and tethered.
    3. Verify that all barriers and nets are installed correctly and in good condition.
    4. Inspect overhead fixtures and ensure they are secure and free of any potential hazards.
    5. Check for any loose or unsecured items in the mast area.
    6. Document the equipment location by inspection area, photograph each item surveyed, include a unique identification number to each item, describe each item surveyed, inspect and document the primary and secondary securing methods.
    7. Use the DROPS Audit checklist to verify the DROPS Management System, the visual mast inspection, and the inspection of the equipment installed at height.
    8. Identify any gaps from industry and client or operator requirements, hazards, and recommendations for remedial actions.
    9. If you find any defects or signs of deterioration on the equipment or the securing methods, do not use them and remove them from service immediately. Replace them with new ones that meet the safety standards and requirements.
